# Phinary

Phinary runs binary prediction markets on the prices of ETH and SOL inside a Uniswap v4 hook. Every market asks one question, such as "ETH above 2,689.62 USD at 15:28 UTC?", and trades through ordinary Uniswap swaps.

![A live Phinary market in the Uniswap app: 5 USDC buys 6.08 ETHUP](/screenshots/uniswap/quote.png)

- **Two tokens per market.** `ETHUP` (YES) pays 1 USDC if ETH ends above the strike. `ETHDOWN` (NO) pays 1 USDC if it does not. The 15-minute and SOL markets use `ETH15MUP`, `SOLUP`, `SOL15MUP` and their `DOWN` pairs.
- **The price is the probability.** A YES price of 0.78 means the market gives YES a 78% chance.
- **Priced by the hook.** A Black-Scholes formula quotes every swap. The pools hold no liquidity, and an LP vault inside the hook takes the other side.
- **Settled by Uniswap.** The strike and the result both come from a Uniswap v4 pool of the asset against USDC. There is no external oracle and no external service.
- **Nobody in charge.** Markets open on fixed schedules that anyone can trigger, and nobody can change a market or choose its outcome.

## Live demo

On Unichain Sepolia four **tracks** run side by side on one hook and one LP vault:

| Track | A new market | Trading stops | Price averaged over |
|---|---|---|---|
| ETH and SOL, 1 minute | Every minute, as the last one expires | 12 s before expiry | The last 10 s |
| ETH and SOL, 15 minutes | Every quarter hour, as the last one expires | 32 s before expiry | The last 30 s |

Each track's markets run back to back, and the result is known a few seconds after expiry. Every market records which track opened it: `schedulerOf(marketId)` on the `MarketGatekeeper`, or the address of the scheduler that emitted its `MarketOpened` event ([Market tracks](/integration#market-tracks)).
