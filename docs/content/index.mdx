# Phinary

Phinary runs binary prediction markets on the price of ETH inside a Uniswap v4 hook. Every market asks one question, such as "ETH above 2,689.62 USD at 15:28 UTC?", and trades through ordinary Uniswap swaps.

![A live Phinary market in the Uniswap app: 5 USDC buys 6.08 ETHUP](/screenshots/uniswap/quote.png)

- **Two tokens per market.** `ETHUP` (YES) pays 1 USDC if ETH ends above the strike. `ETHDOWN` (NO) pays 1 USDC if it does not.
- **The price is the probability.** A YES price of 0.78 means the market gives YES a 78% chance.
- **Priced by the hook.** A Black-Scholes formula quotes every swap. The pools hold no liquidity, and an LP vault inside the hook takes the other side.
- **Settled by Uniswap.** The strike and the result both come from a Uniswap v4 ETH/USDC pool. There are no external oracles and no external services.
- **Nobody in charge.** Markets open on a fixed schedule that anyone can trigger, and nobody can change a market or choose its outcome.

## Live demo

On Unichain Sepolia a new market opens every minute and runs for two minutes, so there is always one to trade. Trading stops 12 seconds before expiry, and the result is known about 2 seconds after it.
