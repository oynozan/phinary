# Trade in the Uniswap app

Phinary markets trade in the normal Uniswap web app. The demo runs a local copy of it in which USDC to YES or NO swaps on Unichain Sepolia are quoted by the hook and sent through UniversalRouter. Every other token and chain works exactly as on app.uniswap.org.

## Before you start

- **A wallet.** MetaMask with a plain account. Smart accounts and EIP-7702 delegated accounts cannot sign the Permit2 message this flow uses.
- **Unichain Sepolia** selected, with a little testnet ETH for gas.
- **Testnet USDC**, Circle's token at `0x31d0…768F`.
- **The app** open at `http://localhost:3000`. It only works on localhost ([run it locally](/guides/run-locally)).

## Buy, sell and collect

<Steps>

### Turn on testnet mode

Connect the wallet, open **Settings** and switch on **Testnet mode**. Stay connected afterwards: disconnecting switches testnet mode off again.

### Pick a market

Put USDC in the **Sell** box and open the token picker in the **Buy** box.

![The token picker lists open markets first and settled markets below](/screenshots/uniswap/token-picker.png)

- **Prediction markets** lists the YES and NO tokens of the newest markets that are still open.
- **Settled prediction markets (sell to redeem)** lists markets that already have a result.

A token name is the whole question. `ETH > $2688.81 26 Sep 15:25` is the YES token (`ETHUP`): it pays 1 USDC if ETH is above 2,688.81 USD at 15:25 UTC. The matching `ETH < …` token (`ETHDOWN`) pays if it is not.

### Read the market card

Once a market token is selected, a card under the swap box shows the market live.

![A 5 USDC quote for ETHUP with the market card below it](/screenshots/uniswap/quote.png)

| On the card | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ETH above 2,689.62 USD at 15:28:00 UTC? | The question. `Trading` means it is open |
| ETH 2,690.24 USD, 0.62 USD above strike | The current price and its distance from the strike |
| Closes in 1:06 | Time left to trade |
| Expires in 1:18 | Time until the result is measured |
| YES 77.9% | The market's probability that YES wins, which is also its fair price |
| Ask 0.819 · Bid 0.740 | You pay the ask per token when buying and get the bid when selling |

### Enter an amount

Type how much USDC to spend. The **Buy** box shows how many tokens you get, and the number refreshes every second.

In the screenshot, 5 USDC buys 6.08285 ETHUP, an average of 0.822 per token. That is a little above the 0.819 ask, because every token bought in the same block moves the price up slightly ([price impact](/concepts/pricing#price-impact)).

### Keep slippage on Auto

![Max slippage set to Auto](/screenshots/uniswap/slippage.png)

A two-minute market moves fast: its price can shift by 10 to 16 cents during a wallet confirmation. **Auto** sizes the tolerance for each quote. A fixed 1 to 5% makes most swaps in short markets revert with `V4TooLittleReceived` after you have signed.

### Confirm the swap

The first buy takes three wallet steps:

1. **Approve USDC** for Permit2, once per token.
2. **Sign** a Permit2 message for UniversalRouter, valid for 30 days.
3. **Swap.**

Later buys need one confirmation. Selling YES or NO needs only **Sign** and **Swap**, because outcome tokens already allow Permit2.

### Sell early, or hold

Until trading closes you can sell back at the bid, by putting the token in **Sell** and USDC in **Buy**. After the cutoff the market leaves the picker until it has a result, about 14 seconds later.

### Collect your winnings

After settlement the market moves to **Settled prediction markets**, and its card shows the result.

![A settled market card: YES won and pays 1.00 USDC](/screenshots/uniswap/settled-card.png)

Sell the winning token for USDC in the same swap box, at exactly **1.00 USDC per token**. The losing token is worth nothing and cannot be sold.

</Steps>

<Callout type="important">
Trade early in a market's life. Trading stops 12 seconds before expiry, and near the end a small ETH move swings the price a lot.
</Callout>

## Example

At 15:26:54 Alice spends 5 USDC on the market in the screenshot and gets 6.08285 ETHUP.

| Result at 15:28:00 | What Alice does | Alice ends with |
|---|---|---|
| ETH's average over the last 10 s is above 2,689.62 | Sells 6.08285 ETHUP at 1.00 | 6.08 USDC, a profit of 1.08 |
| ETH's average is at or below 2,689.62 | Nothing to sell | 0 USDC, a loss of 5 |
